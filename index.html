These are all questions about personal finance that seem daunting to someone who's just starting to handle being self-reliant for the first time.

Life after graduation comes with opportunities and price tags. Does your child know the real costs, fine print, and hidden expenses behind buying a car or a home? When they land their first job, would you like them to understand how a paycheque really works?

The program

A three-day personal finance summer program that gives students the knowledge to make confident money decisions for life. Designed for students entering Grades 9–10, the program covers real-world topics including budgeting, taxes and buying a home, taught by working professionals who live these decisions every day.

Keynote speaker

Jeff MacLean

Senior Vice President of Manufacturing, The Shaw Group
Former President, Michelin North America (Canada) Inc.

Wednesday, June 24, 2026, 7:00 PM

A seasoned industrial leader, Jeff MacLean brings over 30 years of global manufacturing and corporate governance experience to his role. As Senior Vice President of Manufacturing for The Shaw Group, he provides strategic oversight for eight divisions within one of Eastern Canada's leading community developers, residential builders and natural resource manufacturers.

A former President of Michelin North America (Canada) Inc., Mr. MacLean's career began in engineering as a co-op student in 1988 and expanded into international executive leadership in North America and Europe. He is an alumnus of Halifax's Saint Mary's University (Bachelor of Science) and Dalhousie University, where he completed his Engineering degree.

Beyond his professional life, Jeff remains deeply committed to his community, known for coaching youth sports, serving as Chair of Nova Scotia Business Inc., and as a passionate supporter of the Special Olympics. In his leisure time, he enjoys fishing and mountain biking.

Workshop leaders

Financial Literacy Counsel

Mr. Jee-Woon

Financial Advisor and Investment Specialist

Jee-Woon brings specialized experience having provided financial education to Olympic athletes, the UBC School of Medicine, and major healthcare organizations. He empowers young professionals to master foundational money principles, helping them regain total control over their time and energy. A recognized voice in the industry, he has appeared in The Globe and Mail and amassed over half a million views for his digital content on financial literacy.

Budget Formatting

Rosa Lenihan

Founder and Owner, Quantum Academy and Quantum College

Born into a family of educators in Taiwan and a scholarship recipient at SUNY Buffalo, Rosa founded Quantum Academy in 2009 after building a reputation for high standards in Richmond and Vancouver. Her philosophy, that rigorous academics and community responsibility are inseparable, shaped an institution focused on university preparation alongside deep civic engagement.

Buying a House

Paisley Agnesini

REALTOR®, RE/MAX City Realty

Raised in New Westminster within a real estate family, Paisley developed hands-on mastery of property management long before earning her licence from the UBC Sauder School of Business. By 21 she had purchased her first home and successfully house hacked to build early equity. With a background in high-stakes contract negotiation from Vancouver's film industry, she now helps clients build long-term wealth through smart and transparent real estate decisions.

Tax and Finance

Jerry Hu

CPA and Co-founder, Everplus CPA Ltd.

With nearly a decade of public practice experience including time at Ernst & Young, Jerry advises individuals and small businesses on tax compliance, financial reporting, and estate planning. A 2023 graduate of the CPA In-Depth Tax Program, he specializes in Canadian corporate and personal tax planning, regularly supporting corporate reorganizations and succession planning for entrepreneurs and high-net-worth individuals.

In-depth Research

Tracey Lee

M.Ed, Registered Clinical Counsellor

Tracey is a registered clinical counsellor with over 30 years of experience supporting youth and mentoring emerging professionals. Her extensive background in BC's public and independent schools is bolstered by her ability to teach in-depth research from diverse perspectives. Tracey has designed K–12 wellness programs, supervises university interns, and remains a dedicated advocate for student mental health. Beyond her clinical practice, Tracey is a lifelong mentor and elite debate coach, having led Team Canada to a World Schools Debate Championship in 2010. Her community impact has earned her the CSDF McLeese Award, the Vancouver Quadra Hidden Heroes Award, and Stanford University's J.E. Wallace Sterling Award.

Buying a Car

Jeffrey Nelson

Managing Partner, Richmond Hyundai (Harris Auto)

Jeffrey brings 20 years of Canadian automotive experience across both OEM and retail. As Managing Partner at Richmond Hyundai he built it into the top EV dealer in BC, executed a successful buy/sell, and grew market share in under three months. Earlier roles include General Manager at Volkswagen with Go Auto and Auto Canada, where he led two award-winning dealers and a team of 70, and 13.5 years at Mercedes-Benz Canada as Region Operations Manager for Western Canada, overseeing 18 dealers while the brand maintained #1 luxury manufacturer status.
